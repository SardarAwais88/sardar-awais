## Part 1: How to Submit Your Sitemap to Google Search Console (GSC)

Google Search Console is how you tell Google that your site exists and that it needs to be crawled immediately.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. **Go to Google Search Console:** Open [search.google.com/search-console](https://search.google.com/search-console).
2. **Select your Property:** Click on the top-left dropdown and select `https://sardarawais.com` (If you haven't added it, click "Add Property" and verify it via your Hostinger DNS).
3. **Navigate to Sitemaps:** In the left-hand sidebar, under the **"Indexing"** section, click on **"Sitemaps"**.
4. **Enter your Sitemap URL:** In the box that says *“Add a new sitemap”*, type exactly: `sitemap.xml`
5. **Submit:** Click the **"Submit"** button.
6. **Verify:** You should see a success message that says "Sitemap submitted successfully". The status should eventually turn green ("Success").

> [!TIP]
> Googlebot will now crawl all your new service pages and case studies. It may take 3-7 days for the new rankings and changes to fully reflect on Google.

### Tier 2: Community & Forum Backlinks (Do this Daily)
Answer questions on platforms where founders and developers hang out. Don't spam—provide a high-quality answer and leave your link at the bottom.

**Where to post:**
1. **Reddit:** Subreddits like `r/LangChain`, `r/OpenAI`, `r/artificial`, `r/SaaS`.
   - *Example:* Someone asks "How do I give Claude access to my database?" You answer explaining MCP and say, *"I actually do [MCP Server Development](https://sardarawais.com/services/mcp-server-development) for a living, here is how you architect it..."*
2. **Quora:** Search for questions like "How much does it cost to build an AI voice agent?" Answer it thoroughly and link to your Voice Agent service page.
3. **IndieHackers & YCombinator Hacker News:** Share your case studies (e.g., the Dental Clinic voice agent) as a "Show HN" post.

### Tier 3: Guest Posting on Niche AI Newsletters (Do this Monthly)
Find AI newsletters on Substack or Beehiiv that cater to business owners. 
- **Action:** Email the newsletter owner.
- **Pitch:** "Hey! I'm an AI automation engineer. I recently built a voice agent that saved a dental clinic $15k/month. I'd love to write a free guest post for your newsletter breaking down exactly how I built it."
- **The Catch:** In the author bio of the guest post, you get a "Do-Follow" backlink to your homepage.
